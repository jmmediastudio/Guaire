The reference

Take it easy.

Tóg go bog é is Irish for take it easy. On the back it's a green road sign on two poles, the kind you pass on every back road, with a red Temporary surface plate that's been there longer than anyone can remember.

Print

Front: a small white Guaire logo on the chest.

Back: a green road sign on two poles reading Tóg go bog é, GUAIRE, with a red Temporary surface plate underneath.

Fit and fabric

Oversized and boxy, with dropped shoulders and a high crew neck. Black heavyweight cotton.

Care

Wash cold, inside out. Hang to dry. Iron around the print, not on it.
